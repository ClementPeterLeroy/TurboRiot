# Turbo Riot roadmap

City Square initial construction is now implemented within v0.6.1-dev: two stair streets, fountain, coastal docks, gardens and reusable town architecture. It is the fourth gallery circuit; street-time balancing and traffic reliability remain beta tuning work. [Scope, evidence and limitations](city-square.md). Only Legend Park and Riot Arena remain deferred catalog entries; this supersedes the earlier three-deferred-circuit status below without changing milestone numbering.

The canonical current milestone and full agreed plan are maintained in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md). Keep scope changes there to avoid conflicting copies. Circuit 8's [stadium/bridge environment](figure8-stadium.md) has developer approval of its reconstruction/artistic direction. The [final logo/two-pad pass](figure8-final-polish.md) is implemented, pending acceptance; earlier assembly and road study remain archived. No release acceptance is inferred.

**v0.6.1 — Circuit Selection & First Testing Grounds** initially delivered circuit selection, the shared framework and two new beta test circuits without finished environments or scenery: Figure-8 and GCN Yoshi Circuit (DS). It also corrected racing FPS drops and Figure-8 crossing collisions. Subsequent environment and City Square work remains Unreleased as `0.6.1-dev`, pending developer acceptance. Original circuit development and the two remaining deferred educational circuits remain within Testing Grounds; see [implementation](testing-grounds.md). v0.6.0 preserves its Playable Alpha history and Graffiti Speed/Graffiti Impact identity work to v0.7.0. See [Playable Alpha](playable-alpha.md), [versioning](../VERSIONING.md) and the [cumulative checklist](testing/ACCEPTANCE_CHECKLIST.md).
